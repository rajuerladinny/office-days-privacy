title: Office Days — Privacy Policy
---

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

Office Days ("the app") is a personal office-attendance log for Android. This policy explains what the app handles and why. The developer of the app is referred to as "we".

## Summary
- Your attendance data stays **on your phone**. We don't run a server and don't receive your data.
- The app uses your location **only** to detect when you arrive at and leave the office you set up, including when the app is closed or not in use.
- We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

## Data the app uses

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Precise and approximate location, including in the background | Checks whether you are inside your confirmed office boundary so arrival and departure can be recorded automatically | Processed on the phone. Individual location fixes and route history are **not stored** |
| Office pin, boundary radius and tracking settings | Defines your office and attendance rules | Private app storage on your phone |
| Visit times, corrections and their reasons, holidays, leave, company and office names you enter | Your attendance history and reports | Private app storage on your phone |

## Services provided by others
- **Google Play services (Fused Location Provider and Geofencing API)** supply location detection on Android. Their handling is covered by Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **OpenStreetMap tile servers** receive standard web requests (your IP address and the map area requested) **only while you have the office map picker open**. See https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

## Exports
PDF reports are created only when you ask for one, and saved where you choose. If you pick a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file. Exported files aren't password-protected and stay where you saved them until you delete them.

## Backups, retention and deletion
- Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for the app's data.
- Data stays on your phone until you use **Privacy → Delete all app data**, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files must be deleted separately.

## Permissions
- **Location (precise, and "Allow all the time")** for automatic arrival and departure detection. You can pause tracking or revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.
- **Notifications** show tracking status while a visit is being checked.
- **Foreground service (location)** keeps detection running reliably while it confirms a visit.
- **Run at startup** re-registers the office geofence after the phone restarts.
- **Internet** is used only to load map tiles in the office map picker.

## Children
The app is intended for working adults and is not directed at children under 18.

## Changes
We will update this page when the app's data handling changes and revise the date above.
